What You Can Do

Join the Women’s Sports Foundation (WSF) in their fight for gender equity. Your donation will provide the essential ressources they need to defend Title IX, take on critical research, support organizations that encourage girls involvement in sports, and so much more. Your donation is tax-deductible to the full extent of the law.

Other ways to give

The WSF accepts various methods of donation, including charitable vehicles, cash, checks, stocks, IRA, wire transfer, or cryptocurrency. All donations help provide opputunities for young women in sports.
