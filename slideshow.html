Colleges still do not offer athletic opportunities to female athletes proportional to their enrollment. The disparities in salary and media coverage between men’s and women’s sports sets limits on how big young, aspiring female athletes can dream. It’s time for sports media to substantially invest in professional women’s sports. Women’s sports received an average share of 15% of media coverage in 2022. If coverage trends continue at this current rate of growth, women’s share of coverage should be at least 20% by 2025. Millennials represent roughly 30% of all sports fans and 46% of this population turns to streaming platforms to watch sports events.
